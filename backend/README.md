Overview

Kiosk AI is an AI agent designed to help micro, small, and medium-scale businesses manage customer communication effortlessly. It acts as a smart front-desk that handles customer inquiries, feedback, and follow-ups across messaging platforms, allowing business owners to stay responsive without being overwhelmed.

Instead of replacing human staff, Kiosk AI supports them by handling repetitive conversations, identifying important issues, and surfacing insights that matter.

Motivation

Many small businesses rely heavily on online messaging platforms to interact with customers. As demand grows, responding to messages consistently becomes difficult. Important inquiries are missed, feedback goes unanswered, and customer satisfaction drops.

Kiosk AI was inspired by a real-world challenge faced by a small business owner whose staff could not keep up with increasing online customer interactions. This solution was built to support MSMEs facing similar challenges by providing reliable, always-on customer communication assistance.

Problem Statement

Micro and small businesses struggle with:

High volumes of customer messages

Delayed or inconsistent responses

Poor feedback tracking

Lack of structured customer insights

Limited staff availability outside business hours

These issues directly impact customer trust, retention, and business growth.

Proposed Solution

Kiosk AI introduces an AI agent that integrates directly into customer messaging channels and functions as a business assistant. It handles routine communication, detects critical issues, and provides business owners with visibility into customer interactions.

What Kiosk AI Does

Responds to customer inquiries automatically

Handles FAQs and basic support requests

Identifies complaints and urgent issues

Logs conversations for CRM purposes

Alerts business owners when human attention is needed

Operates continuously without downtime

How It Works

A customer sends a message through a supported platform

The message is forwarded to Kiosk AI via a webhook

The AI agent processes the message using business context and conversation history

An appropriate response is sent back to the customer

Important interactions are logged and flagged for the business owner when necessary

The AI agent remains platform-agnostic, allowing future expansion to multiple communication channels.

Current Implementation

For rapid deployment and demonstration, Kiosk AI is currently integrated using:

Messaging bot interface for real-time conversations

Backend AI agent with conversation memory

Basic CRM data storage for messages and users
